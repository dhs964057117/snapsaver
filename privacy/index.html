Privacy Policy

SnapSaver Browser for Android · Effective date: August 8, 2026

Overview

SnapSaver Browser ("SnapSaver", "the app") is a privacy-focused Android browser with a built-in media sniffer and download manager. This policy explains what information the app and this website handle. The short version: we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. SnapSaver is designed so that what you browse and download stays on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

SnapSaver does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. We do not collect or receive your browsing history, search queries, downloads, location, contacts, or device identifiers on any server we operate, because we operate no such server-side collection.

Data Stored on Your Device

To function, the app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Files you choose to download (videos, music, images, documents).
  • Browsing history, cookies and cache, which you can delete at any time and which are wiped automatically when you use Incognito mode.
  • Bookmarks, settings and preferences such as your chosen language.

This local data never leaves your device through us. Deleting the app removes it.

This Website

This website (snapsaver.suanss.com) is a static informational site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies for tracking. Your browser stores only simple preferences (theme and language) in local storage. GitHub may process standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as part of hosting; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through Google Play, which is operated by Google. When you install, update or purchase, Google may process data according to the Google Privacy Policy. The app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob; AdMob may use the advertising ID of your device to measure and serve ads. You can limit ad personalization in your Android settings.

Permissions

SnapSaver requests only the permissions needed to deliver its features, primarily network access and file storage access so downloads can be saved where you expect them. Downloads you export are written to your shared storage by your own action.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the developer through the contact email listed on the SnapSaver Google Play listing.
