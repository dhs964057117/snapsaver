Privacy Information

SnapSaver Browser for Android · Website summary updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

SnapSaver is an Android browser with media detection, downloads, and a media player. This page summarizes privacy information for the app and this website. The full app policy linked from SnapSaver is available in English and Chinese. Please read that policy for the complete app disclosures.

Accounts and Browsing

You do not need a SnapSaver account to start browsing. Sites you visit may require their own sign-in and process data under their own policies. Incognito mode controls local browsing records; it does not make network activity anonymous to websites or network providers.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores data on your device to deliver browser and download features, including:

  • Files you choose to download (videos, music, images, documents).
  • Browsing history, cookies and cache, managed through the browser's privacy controls.
  • Bookmarks, settings and preferences such as your chosen language.

Files that you export or share can be accessed by the apps or destinations you choose. Preserve important downloads before clearing app data or uninstalling.

This Website

This website (snapsaver.suanss.com) is a static informational site served through Cloudflare. The site code does not include advertising or analytics scripts. It stores a theme preference in local storage. Hosting and network providers may process request information to deliver and protect the site. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which sends requests to Google. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through Google Play, which is operated by Google. When you install, update or purchase, Google may process data according to the Google Privacy Policy. The app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob; AdMob may use the advertising ID of your device to measure and serve ads. The app privacy policy also describes Firebase analytics and the optional weather feature, which can share location with the weather provider. Review the full app policy and in-app consent and privacy controls for details.

Permissions

SnapSaver requests only the permissions needed to deliver its features, primarily network access and file storage access so downloads can be saved where you expect them. Optional weather features can request location permission. Downloads you export are written to shared storage by your action.

Further Information

The full app privacy policy covers children's privacy, data processing, permissions, and ways to contact the developer. The Google Play listing also provides the app's Data safety information.

Changes to This Policy

This website summary may be updated when the website or app information changes. Refer to the full app policy for its effective date and complete terms.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the developer through the contact email listed on the SnapSaver Google Play listing.
